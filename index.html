  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

3. Chart Image Processing & AI Analysis

  • Chart images are transmitted over encrypted TLS connections.
  • Images are processed by third-party AI vision models under contract with us, solely for the purpose of generating your analysis.
  • Analysis results are stored against your account so you can review history.
  • The original uploaded image is temporarily cached during processing and discarded shortly afterward; it is not retained as a permanent record on our servers.
  • AI-generated output is informational and educational. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any financial instrument.

7. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is transmitted over TLS/SSL.
  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes; we never store plaintext passwords.
  • API access is authenticated using signed JWT tokens with short expirations.
  • Production servers are protected by firewalls, network-level access controls, and intrusion-detection monitoring.
  • Access to user data by our staff is restricted on a need-to-know basis and logged.

No system is 100% secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as required to provide the App and comply with legal obligations. After account deletion, your data is removed from our active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days. Anonymized, aggregated statistics that cannot be linked back to you may be retained for product analytics.

8.1 How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can permanently delete your Forex Trade AI account and all associated personal data at any time:

  • In the App: open Settings → Account → Delete Account. You will be taken to a secure web page (https://masterai.trade/delete) to confirm your identity and complete the deletion.
  • By email: send a deletion request from your registered email address to privacy@masterai.trade. We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.
Account deletion is irreversible. It removes your profile, chart images, analysis history, signal records, and wallet balance. Subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play must be cancelled separately from your Apple ID or Google Play account settings — deleting your Forex Trade AI account does not automatically cancel an active subscription.
